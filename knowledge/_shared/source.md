# Source: knowledge/_shared

Material every Microsoft 365 workload needs and none owns: licensing and
service limits, Entra ID identity and roles, Microsoft Graph fundamentals,
and the admin center map.

This directory exists so that adding Teams, Exchange, or Power Platform
later does not require duplicating or relocating shared material.

## Review policy

Licensing content dates fastest. Treat any license claim older than six
months as requiring verification, and anything older than twelve months as
unusable until rechecked. Per `AGENTS.md`, say so in the answer rather than
presenting stale material as current.
